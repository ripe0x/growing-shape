'P(reach before won)' is for the FIRST featured of each path. Reaching 50/100 requires the featured to survive uncomposed-by-win across tens of thousands of draws, which is rare.

Mean featured wins per path over 60000 draws: 9.5. E[backing of winning featured]: 3.81 ETH.

## 10->50 stagnation

10->50 needs 40.004 ETH of growth. At ~0.00235 ETH/draw that is ~17,023 draws (~9 days at 2000/day). The 10->50 and 50->100 steps are fixed by the Shapes ladder, so the only lever is the growth RATE (higher markup) or accepting that 50/100 are rare, multi-year outcomes.
